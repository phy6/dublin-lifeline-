Dublin Street Essentials

📞 Homeless Freephone (DRHE): 1800 707 707
ℹ️ Street Resource Guide & Advice

Power & Wi-Fi: Central Library (ILAC) and Pearse St Library provide free seating, outlets, and Wi-Fi during operating hours without requiring a card to sit.

Water: Dublin City Council public water refill points are accessible 24/7 along key civic plazas.

Emergency Shelter: DRHE Freephone (1800 707 707) opens daily at 10:00 AM. Call early for placement requests.
