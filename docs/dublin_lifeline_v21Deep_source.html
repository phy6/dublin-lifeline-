👕 Clothes, Hygiene & Essential Supplies
  • Capuchin Day Centre: Free clothing distribution and hygiene packs available on‑site during week mornings.
  • Merchants Quay Ireland (MQI): Warm showers, toiletries, and emergency adult clothing provisions.
  • The Light House (Tiglin): Winter outerwear, shoes, distribution of hygiene kits, and thermal items.
  • St. Vincent de Paul (SVP): Clothing vouchers for local SVP shops or direct support upon casework inquiry.
📚 Public Libraries & Free Workspaces
  • Central Library (Ilac Centre): Free Wi‑Fi, desk seating, power outlets, research resources, and computer stations.
  • Pearse Street Library: Desk space, public internet, quiet reading zones, and printing facilities.
  • Charleville Mall Library: Neighbourhood hub offering study seating, PC access, printing, and scanning.
🚨 Emergency Night Accommodation (DRHE)

Freephone Line: Call 1800 707 707 daily at 10:00 AM to register for emergency night beds.

  • First‑time presentations: Attend Central Placement Service in person (Parkgate Hall, 6–9 Conyngham Rd, Dublin 8).
  • Requirements: PPS Number, photo ID (if available), and local connection details.
📞 Call DRHE now 📞 Simon Community 📞 Focus Ireland

If DRHE is unavailable or lines are full, Dublin Simon Community and Focus Ireland can advise on alternative routes — this app cannot check bed availability itself.

🏛️ Government Welfare & DSP (Intreo)
  • Supplementary Welfare Allowance (SWA): Immediate financial support via your local Intreo Centre or Community Welfare Officer (CWO).
  • Urgent Needs Payment: One‑off emergency payments for essential food, clothing, or deposits.
🆘 Concerned About Someone Sleeping Rough?

This app doesn't collect or store location reports about individuals — that data would have nowhere safe to go. If you're worried about someone sleeping rough in Dublin, contact a real outreach team directly so a trained worker can follow up:

  • Dublin Simon Community Outreach: 01 671 0233
  • Merchants Quay Ireland: 01 524 0924
  • Out of hours / urgent welfare concern: Contact An Garda Síochána on the non-emergency line, or 999/112 if there is immediate danger to life.

Have the person's location, description, and any access notes (e.g. doorway, laneway) ready when you call — that's exactly what the outreach team will ask for.
